            <div class="hero-kicker">Cognitive meta-learning · v19 → v22 “The Editable Scientist”</div>
            <h1>A machine-learning system that <em>remembers, reasons, and can be corrected.</em></h1>
            <p>FIGPSO UIL (Universal Intelligence Layer) wraps standard classifiers — XGBoost, RandomForest, KNN, SymbolicFormula — in a cognitive layer that watches every run, mines causal rules about why it succeeds or fails, stores them as editable knowledge, and applies them to future runs. No human labeling required — but human correction is always possible.</p>

=====FRAG=====

            <div class="hero-kicker">AI · neuroscience · symbolic science</div>
            <h1>A cognitive layer that <em>thinks in graphs, learns in symbols, and yields to human correction.</em></h1>
            <p>FIGPSO UIL wraps classifiers in a living knowledge network — neurons (principles), synapses (causal edges), episodic memory, and an event-sourced scientist loop. Every belief is inspectable, editable, and replayable.</p>
